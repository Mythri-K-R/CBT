BROCHURE DESIGN PROMPT — ExamSphere by Mitra Softwares
========================================================

Create a professional 4-page printed brochure (A4 size, portrait orientation, 210mm × 297mm).
Output format: PDF or high-resolution print-ready image (300 DPI minimum). NOT a PowerPoint/PPTX.

CRITICAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — READ BEFORE STARTING:
- LIGHT THEME throughout — bright, airy, clean. Backgrounds are white or very light.
- IMAGES dominate every page — minimum 55–70% of each page area must be photographs or
  visuals. Text lives in overlay bands, caption strips, or compact content panels.
- Think magazine editorial meets corporate brochure — image-first, text-second.
- Images must be sourced/generated to show real-feeling Indian context:
    • Indian students (17–22 yrs, South Asian appearance) at computers, celebrating results,
      in coaching institute computer labs, holding NEET/JEE books
    • Indian faculty/teacher (30–50 yrs) reviewing tests on a laptop or whiteboard
    • Modern coaching institute / junior college exteriors and smart classrooms
    • Close-ups of a laptop/desktop screen showing a multiple-choice CBT exam interface
    • Group of happy Indian students (mixed gender) in casual or uniform attire
    • Aspirational future-doctors / future-engineers imagery (stethoscope, engineering gear)
- Colour palette — LIGHT THEME:
    Primary:   #FFFFFF  — White (main backgrounds)
    Accent 1:  #1A56DB  — Bright Royal Blue (headings, icon fills, accent bars)
    Accent 2:  #F59E0B  — Warm Amber/Saffron (highlight pills, CTA buttons, call-outs)
    Accent 3:  #16A34A  — Success Green (badges, achievement tags only)
    Text:      #1E293B  — Dark Slate (body text)
    Sub-text:  #64748B  — Medium Grey (captions, sub-headings)
    Card bg:   #F0F4FF  — Very light blue-white (feature cards, info boxes)
- Typography:
    Headings:  Poppins ExtraBold / Montserrat Black
    Sub-heads: Poppins SemiBold
    Body:      Nunito Regular / Open Sans Regular — 10–11pt
    Captions:  Nunito Italic — 9pt
- Thin Royal Blue (#1A56DB) horizontal rule separates major sections
- Small ExamSphere wordmark + "by Mitra Softwares" in footer of every page (grey, 8pt)
- NO dark full-bleed backgrounds — even the cover uses a bright image, not a dark solid colour
- All overlay text areas use a WHITE semi-transparent frosted panel, not a dark overlay
